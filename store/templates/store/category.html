FAQ

1 How are the fish packed for delivery?

Each guppy is packed individually in airtight bags, placed in thermocol boxes, and clearly labeled “Live Fish” for safe handling during transit.

2 What should I do if fish arrive dead or injured?

Please share an unboxing video within 2 hours of delivery. Based on your location, you’ll receive either a 70% refund or a free replacement (you only pay for shipping).

3 Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?

No, we do not offer COD. We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) and direct bank transfers only for secure and verified payments.

4 I’m new to fish keeping. Can I still buy?

Absolutely! We guide beginners in choosing the right guppies and setting up a healthy tank environment. Feel free to reach out anytime.

5 Do you ship to remote and North-East regions?

Yes, we deliver across India, including North-East and remote areas. Please note: refund policy for these regions is 50% or free replacement (shipping applies).

6 How do I join your WhatsApp group or get updates?

Click the “Join WhatsApp Group” button on our website or contact us directly. We regularly share updates, stock info, and helpful fish-keeping tips.
