Shipping & Delivery

We ship live guppies with the utmost care across India. Our packaging and shipping methods ensure that your fish arrive healthy and stress-free.

1. Delivery Coverage

  • ✅ We ship to all major cities, towns, and even remote areas across India
  • ✅ North & North-Eastern states are also covered, but with special refund terms
  • 🚫 International shipping is currently not available

2. Packaging Details

  • 📦 Fish are packed individually in airtight bags
  • 📦 Securely placed in thermocol boxes to maintain temperature
  • 📦 Marked with “Live Fish” stickers for careful handling by couriers
  • 🧊 Ice gel packs included during hotter months to ensure safety

3. Shipping Partners & Timeline

  • 🚚 We use reliable third-party couriers (DTDC, speed post, delhivery, etc.)
  • 📍 Estimated delivery: 24–72 hours from dispatch depending on your location
  • 📲 Tracking details will be shared via order tracking page after dispatch

4. Customer Responsibilities

  • 📬 Provide accurate delivery address and phone number at checkout
  • 📦 Be available to collect the parcel as soon as it arrives
  • 📹 Record an unboxing video immediately after receiving the shipment (required for claims)

5. Important Notes

  • ❌ We do not offer COD – all shipments are prepaid only
  • ✅ We aim to dispatch orders within 1–2 business days after payment and order confirmation
  • ⚠️ Delays due to weather, lockdowns, or courier issues are outside our control
