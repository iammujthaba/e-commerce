Shipping Information

How are fish packed for courier?

Each fish is packed individually in airtight bags, placed inside thermocol boxes, and labeled “Live Fish” for careful handling.

What if the fish arrive dead or injured?

Share your unboxing video with us. You’ll receive a 70% refund of the fish amount (or 50% refund for North India & Northeast), or a free replacement (you pay only the shipping charge).

When should I collect my parcel?

Please collect your parcel as soon as it arrives to avoid any delays or stress on the fish.

Do you ship to North-Eastern and remote areas?

Yes, we ship across India including remote and North-Eastern regions. Please note refund terms differ slightly (50% refund for North & NE).

Orders and Returns

How do I track my order?

Tracking details will be shared via order tracking page after dispatch. You can also contact us anytime for updates.

What if I haven’t received my order?

If your parcel is delayed or missing, please contact us immediately. We'll coordinate with the courier partner and assist you with updates or compensation.

Do you offer refunds or replacements?

Yes. If fish arrive dead, share an unboxing video immediately. You can choose a 70% refund (or 50% for North/Northeast) or request a free replacement (shipping charges apply).

Payments

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc..), UPI ID transfers, and direct bank transfers, you can find all peyment method in our website. COD is not available.

Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available?

No, we do not offer COD. All orders must be prepaid using secure digital payment methods.
